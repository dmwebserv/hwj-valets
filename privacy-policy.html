Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Who this covers

This policy covers HWJ Valets ("we", "us"), a mobile car valeting and detailing service based in Colchester, covering Essex and parts of Suffolk. Contact us on 07926 517296 or harry@hwjvalets.co.uk. If you contact us for a quote or booking, this explains what personal data we collect and what happens to it.

What we collect

If you use the enquiry form, call, WhatsApp or message us on Facebook or Instagram, we collect whatever you give us: your name, phone number, email address, location, vehicle details and the job you need doing.

What we use it for

  • Getting back to you with a quote
  • Arranging and carrying out valeting or detailing work you've booked
  • Keeping basic job and contact records for repeat customers

We don't use your data for marketing you haven't asked for, and we don't sell it to anyone.

Who we share it with

  • FormSubmit.co - processes the enquiry form and forwards submissions to our email.
  • GitHub Pages - hosts and serves this website, and may log standard technical data (like IP address) as part of normal web hosting.
  • Google - via Google Tag Manager. When you allow analytics or advertising cookies, Google Ads and Google Analytics may receive limited data (for example, how you found us) to measure ad and site performance. When consent is denied, these tags work in restricted mode and do not store advertising or analytics cookies.

How long we keep it

Enquiry details are kept only as long as needed to respond to you or deliver a booked job. If you become a repeat customer, we keep basic contact details for as long as we're valeting for you.

Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies to run the site and measure how our ads perform:

  • Cookie consent (hwj_cookie_consent) - remembers your cookie choices for 12 months so we don't ask again. It contains only your chosen settings.
  • Google Tag Manager and Google tags - loads Google Ads and Google Analytics tags. These only run after you choose to allow them; when consent is denied they work in restricted mode and don't store advertising or analytics cookies.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics - privacy-friendly, cookieless visit counting, which doesn't identify individual visitors.

You can change your choices at any time using the "Cookie settings" link in the footer. Your choices are stored in your browser and apply to this site only.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Call 07926 517296 or email harry@hwjvalets.co.uk and we'll sort it out.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page occasionally as the site changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
