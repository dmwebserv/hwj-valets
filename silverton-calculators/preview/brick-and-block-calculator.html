Use our free brick and block calculator to estimate how many bricks or blocks you need for a wall, along with the sand and cement required to mix the mortar. Enter your wall dimensions, choose a standard UK brick (215 x 65mm) or standard block (440 x 215mm) — or enter a custom size — and the calculator does the rest. It suits everything from a small garden wall to a full extension, and you can order bricks, blocks, sand and cement from Silverton Builders Merchants for free local delivery across Essex, Suffolk, Norfolk and Cambridgeshire when you spend over £20.

How to use the brick & block calculator

  1. Choose Single Skin Wall for a half-brick wall (one 100mm leaf) or Double Skin Wall for a wall built with two leaves.
  2. Enter the length and height of your wall in metres. For L-shaped walls, calculate each section separately and add the results together.
  3. Select your material: Standard Brick (215 x 65mm), Standard Block (440 x 215mm) or Custom.
  4. If you chose Custom, enter the face width and height of the brick or block in millimetres.
  5. Press Calculate. The results table shows the number of bricks or blocks, the sand required in kilograms with a breakdown by bag size, and the cement in kilograms and 25kg bags.

What the brick & block calculator assumes

  • Standard brick calculations use 60 bricks per m² of wall, laid with a 10mm mortar joint — the standard UK rate.
  • Standard block calculations use 10 blocks per m² for a 440 x 215mm block, again with 10mm joints.
  • A double skin wall doubles the material count because two leaves are built.
  • Mortar quantities assume roughly 1kg of sand and 1/6kg of cement per brick, or 2kg of sand and 0.4kg of cement per block.
  • Sand is converted to bags at 850kg per bulk bag, 425kg per mini bulk bag and 25kg per small plastic bag.
  • Cement is converted to 25kg bags.
  • Custom sizes divide the wall area by the face area of the unit, with no mortar joint allowance included.
  • No allowance for wastage, breakage or cutting is included — most builders add 5–10%.

Frequently asked questions

How many bricks do I need per square metre?

Allow 60 standard bricks per square metre of single skin wall, laid with 10mm mortar joints. A double skin wall needs about 120 bricks per square metre because it is built with two leaves.

How much sand and cement do I need for 1,000 bricks?

Roughly 1 tonne of sand and 170kg of cement — about seven 25kg bags. The calculator converts these quantities into bulk bags, mini bulk bags and small bags for you.

What is the difference between single skin and double skin?

A single skin (half brick) wall is one 100mm-thick leaf, typical of garden walls and outbuildings. A double skin wall is built with two leaves, usually with a cavity between them, and needs twice as many bricks, sand and cement.

How many blocks do I need per square metre?

Ten standard 440 x 215mm blocks per square metre of single skin wall. Choosing double skin doubles the number of blocks and the mortar materials.

Should I add extra bricks for wastage?

Yes. Add 5–10% for breakage and cutting, and a little more if you are matching an existing bond pattern or ordering special shaped bricks.
